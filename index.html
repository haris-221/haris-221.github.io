Password Strength Checker

A Python password strength estimator inspired by zxcvbn that works out how many guesses an attacker would need, rather than just checking length and character types. It spots common words, years, keyboard patterns and repeats, then gives a 0–4 score, online/offline crack times and feedback on the weak parts.
